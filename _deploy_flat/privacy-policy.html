Who we are

Dr. D'Souza's is a unit of KSAC Hospitals (I) Pvt. Ltd. You can reach us at support email.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you order or contact us, such as your name, email, phone number and delivery address.
  • Order details, such as the products you buy.
  • Basic website usage data through cookies and analytics tools tools used.

Payments are processed by our payment partner provider. We do not store your full card details.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To reply to your questions.
  • To send marketing messages, only if you have agreed to receive them.

Who we share it with

Only with the partners needed to run our store, such as delivery, payment and technology providers. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe from marketing at any time, by contacting support email.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
