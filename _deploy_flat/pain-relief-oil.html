    Our philosophy towards pain

    Relieving pain is important. Understanding why it exists is even more important.

    Pain should never simply be ignored, or merely silenced. Musculoskeletal discomfort can sometimes be a sign of an underlying condition that requires proper evaluation and treatment. Treating pain may help you feel better today. Treating its cause helps you move better tomorrow.

    When should you seek medical advice?

    If your pain
    • Persists despite rest or self-care
    • Frequently returns
    • Is severe or progressively worsening
    • Limits your movement or daily activities
    • Is accompanied by swelling, numbness or other concerning symptoms

    We encourage you to consult a qualified healthcare professional. Our products are designed to support everyday comfort and recovery, but they are not intended to replace professional medical assessment when it is needed.
